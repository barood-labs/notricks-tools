Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

  • Your files are never uploaded. All PDF and image processing and QR code generation happens inside your browser on your device. We have no server that receives your files.
  • We do not require an account and do not collect names, emails or file contents.
  • Your language preference is stored in your browser's local storage only.
  • The hosting provider may keep standard access logs (IP address, browser type) for security, as with any website.
  • If advertising or analytics are added in the future, this page will be updated first and, where required by law (e.g. GDPR), consent will be requested before any cookies are set.
  • QR codes created here are static: the content is encoded directly in the image, so they do not pass through our servers and never expire.

  • ملفاتك لا تُرفع أبدًا. تتم جميع عمليات معالجة ملفات PDF والصور وإنشاء رموز QR داخل متصفحك وعلى جهازك، وليس لدينا أي خادم يستقبل ملفاتك.
