A free remove.bg alternative, now that the remove.bg website is closing

According to the official remove.bg FAQ (remove.bg/faq), "the standalone website will no longer be available from 1 December 2026 at 9:00am CET", background removal moves into Canva, the self-service API moves to Leonardo.Ai, and unused pay-as-you-go credits expire on 1 December 2026. Existing Enterprise API contracts are not affected. If you use Canva already, that is the official path. If you just want cutouts without an account, No-Tricks Tools is a free option that runs on your own device.

How to use it

  1. Use any remaining remove.bg PAYG credits before 1 December 2026, as remove.bg recommends.
  2. Open our Background Remover and try a few of your usual photos.
  3. Compare the edges yourself and keep using whichever tool works best for you.

Features

  • Free full-resolution PNG, no credits, no watermark
  • Photos are processed on your device, never uploaded
  • Product (white background), ID photo and sticker presets plus a touch-up brush
  • Optional $4 one-time Pro for batch-to-ZIP (no subscription)

Honest limitations

Honest comparison: remove.bg is a mature commercial service and can handle difficult hair, fur and backlit portraits better than an open model running on a phone. We have not run a formal side-by-side test against remove.bg. We also have no API, no Photoshop plugin and no team accounts. Our tool needs a one-time model download (114–489 MB) and is slower on phones without WebGPU.

Frequently asked questions

When does remove.bg shut down?

The remove.bg FAQ says the standalone website will no longer be available from 1 December 2026 at 9:00 CET. Check remove.bg/faq for the latest wording.

What happens to my remove.bg credits?

The remove.bg FAQ says unused PAYG credits expire on 1 December 2026. We cannot transfer credits.

Where does remove.bg recommend I go?

Canva for single images and bulk batches, and Leonardo.Ai for API and automated use, per the remove.bg FAQ. Enterprise contracts continue.

Is No-Tricks Tools affiliated with remove.bg or Canva?

No. We are an independent project. remove.bg and Canva are trademarks of their owners.

Do you have an API?

No. If you need an API, remove.bg points to Leonardo.Ai.
