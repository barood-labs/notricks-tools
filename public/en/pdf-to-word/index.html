How to use it

  1. Open the converter and choose your PDF (or drag it onto the page).
  2. Wait a few seconds while the text, headings, tables and images are extracted.
  3. Click Download to save the .docx file and open it in Word, Google Docs or LibreOffice.

Features

  • Keeps paragraphs, headings, font sizes, bold and italic text
  • Detects simple tables and keeps embedded images
  • Supports Arabic and other right-to-left text
  • Works offline once the page has loaded

Honest limitations

Complex layouts (multiple columns, text boxes, exact fonts) are approximated, not copied exactly. Scanned PDFs are photos of pages and contain no text; this tool does not do OCR yet.

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no trial, no daily limit and no watermark. An optional one-time Pro purchase only removes ads.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Conversion happens in your browser with JavaScript. You can disconnect from the internet after the page loads and it still works.

Why is some formatting different?

PDF stores positioned characters, not paragraphs. We rebuild the structure from positions, which works well for normal documents but cannot reproduce every layout.

Does it work with Arabic PDFs?

Yes. Right-to-left lines are detected and written as right-to-left paragraphs in Word.

Can it convert scanned PDFs?

Not yet. Scanned pages are images with no text layer, so there is nothing to extract without OCR.
