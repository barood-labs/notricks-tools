How to use it

  1. Tap Take photo (on a phone) or Upload images.
  2. Check the detected page edges and drag the corners if needed.
  3. Choose a filter (Color, Grayscale, B&W document or Enhance), reorder or delete pages, then export to PDF or JPG.

Features

  • Automatic page edge detection and perspective correction
  • Manual corner adjustment by drag
  • Four filters including a crisp black-and-white document mode
  • Multi-page PDF (A4 or original size) and JPG export, never watermarked

Honest limitations

Edge detection works best with the page on a contrasting background and good light. The scanner engine (about 3–4 MB) downloads the first time you open the tool. Text recognition (OCR) is not included yet.

Frequently asked questions

Is there really no watermark?

Yes. Exports are never watermarked, on the free version too.

Do I need to install an app?

No. It works in your phone or computer browser. You can add the site to your home screen.

Are my scans uploaded?

No. Detection, cropping, filters and PDF creation all happen on your device.

What if the edges are detected wrongly?

Tap Corners and drag the four circles to the page corners, or choose Whole image.

Can I scan multi-page documents?

Yes. Add as many pages as you like, reorder or delete them, and export one PDF.
