How to use it

  1. Choose a compression level (Recommended works for most files).
  2. Choose your PDF.
  3. Compare the before/after size and download the smaller file.

Features

  • Up to 90–99% smaller for scanned PDFs
  • Three quality levels
  • Shows before and after size
  • Tells you honestly when your file is already optimized

Honest limitations

Pages are converted to images, so text in the result is no longer selectable. Best for scans and photos; text-only PDFs are usually already small.

Frequently asked questions

How much smaller will my PDF get?

Scanned PDFs often shrink by 80–99%. Text-only PDFs may not shrink at all, and we tell you when that happens.

Will the quality drop?

Slightly at the Strong level. Light keeps the best quality.

Why is the text no longer selectable?

Pages are re-rendered as compressed images. Keep your original if you need selectable text.

Is my file uploaded?

No, compression runs in your browser.
